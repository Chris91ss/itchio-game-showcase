### Collection of Games and Applications on itch.io

This repository highlights a curated selection of my game development and software projects, all available on [itch.io](https://chris91s.itch.io/). Each project offers unique experiences, from intense gameplay and strategic challenges to educational tools. Below are the featured creations with brief descriptions.

### Featured Games and Applications:

1. **[Gehennam](https://chris91s.itch.io/gehennam)**  
   **Genre:** Platformer  
   **Description:** Embark on a harrowing journey through hell as Dante, where you'll face demonic foes, navigate treacherous landscapes, and overcome dangerous traps to rescue your beloved. Prepare for an intense platforming experience filled with fast-paced action and emotional storytelling.

2. **[Survivalis Mortuorum](https://chris91s.itch.io/survivalis-mortuorum)**  
   **Genre:** Survival  
   **Description:** Fight for your life in this top-down zombie survival game. Navigate eerie environments, upgrade your arsenal, and fend off relentless waves of undead as you struggle to survive in a bleak, post-apocalyptic world.

3. **[Defend The Castle](https://chris91s.itch.io/defend-the-castle)**  
   **Genre:** Strategy, Clicker  
   **Description:** Gather resources, fortify your defenses, and survive waves of enemies in this fast-paced clicker defense game. Strategically build and upgrade your castle to withstand increasingly powerful foes as you expand your stronghold.

4. **[Base Converter and Calculator App](https://chris91s.itch.io/base-converter-calculator)**  
   **Type:** Utility  
   **Description:** A comprehensive tool designed for converting numbers between numerical bases and performing arithmetic operations in bases from 2 to 16. Ideal for developers, students, or anyone working with binary, octal, hexadecimal, or other custom base systems.  
   **Key Features:**  
   - Conversion methods: Base 10 intermediary, rapid conversions, substitution, and successive divisions.  
   - Arithmetic operations: Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division in any base.  
   - Documentation: Includes pseudocode explanations of conversion and calculation logic.  

5. **[Pathfinder: A* Visualization](https://chris91s.itch.io/pathfinder-visualization)**  
   **Type:** Educational Tool  
   **Description:** An interactive demonstration of the A* pathfinding algorithm, featuring real-time updates, debug views, and interactive grid-based map manipulation. Perfect for learning or teaching pathfinding principles in game development.  
   **Key Features:**  
   - Place obstacles and move targets to see recalculations.  
   - Debug view with node costs and heuristics.  
   - Controls: Left-click to move, right-click to place obstacles, and Enter to toggle debug mode.  

6. **[Leaping Souls](https://chris91s.itch.io/leaping-souls)**  
   **Genre:** Precision Platformer  
   **Description:** A tough-as-nails precision platformer where every jump counts. Ascend a towering world, mastering each leap to avoid plummeting back to the start. With no checkpoints, it's a test of persistence and precision.  
   **Key Features:**  
   - Challenging gameplay with precise mechanics.  
   - High stakes with no checkpoints.  
   - Retro visuals and atmospheric sound design.  

7. **[Metroidvania Demo](https://chris91s.itch.io/metroidvania-demo)**  
   **Genre:** Platformer, Action  
   **Description:** A rich blend of exploration and combat in a feature-packed platformer. Includes an inventory system, advanced AI with behavior trees, and mechanics like parrying, double jumps, dashing, ledge climbing, and more.  
   **Key Features:**  
   - Advanced combat system with parry mechanics.  
   - Smart AI using pathfinding and behavior trees.  
   - Intricate levels with hidden areas and challenging platforming.
